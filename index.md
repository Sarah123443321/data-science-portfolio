## About Me
I am Sarah Spielbauer, a junior at UNC Charlotte. I am originally from Minnesota and moved to Charlotte for college. I am pursuing a double major in psychology and data science with a minor in statistics. I am primarily interested in helping adolescent mental health research through understanding data and patterns. My coursework in data science has helped me to learn data analysis, ethical research methods and coding programs. I hope to continue building an understanding of the ethical implementations of research and understand how to use data in a respectful manner. While my coursework in psychology has helped me to understand the cognitive processes of decision making and the biological factors that contribute to mental health. I am primarily interested in clinical psychology. Outside of coursework my experience managing a youth summer camp taught me skills in on the spot problem solving as well as communication and managing a team. I worked directly with campers of varying age and cognitive abilities. I was able to adapt program structure to fit a variety of needs which helped me to better understand how cognition plays a role in behavior. Furthermore, studying abroad in Florence, spring of 2026 taught me flexibility and the importance of global perspectives. I think having an understanding of diverse perspectives helps you to challenge your own narrative and better understand how to represent a variety of people. I think this is incredibly beneficial when it comes to data and understanding patterns in behavior. I strive to continue building these fundamentals and grow my foundation to be a strong and ethical data researcher. I hope to learn ethical limitations, and understand how to demonstrate this through findings to clearly communicate to wide varieties of people. Ultimately, I aim to combine quantitative data science with psychological research to analyze trends in human behavior, communicate findings clearly to diverse audiences, and advocate for adolescent mental health in underrepresented communities.
