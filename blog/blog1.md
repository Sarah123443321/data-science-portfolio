What is Data Science to Me?

Data science to me is using and translating data into something usable that can be manipulated to someones benefit. Data is collected everywhere both known and unknown. It is our job to understand how it is collected, manipulated and explained. As a data scientist it is our responsibility to understand how to collect and use data ethically to not harm. Data is collected in all kinds of ways that people may not know they have agreed to. Such as through social media, apps tracking our location, clicks on amazon. All these our ways our data is collected. The data is then used, when it is used it can be sold to companies to create informational profiles that can then be used to direct ads. A lot of data collected about people is private and has bias. It is important to understand what data is ethical to be collected, vs what data should not be collected. In this course I hope to better understand the ethical uses of data science. I hope to learn the ways in which data can be used to help rather then harm and how to figure out what data is harmful.
