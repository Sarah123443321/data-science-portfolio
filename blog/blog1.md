What is Data Science to Me?


Data Science to me is translating raw data into something usable and understandable. It is a multidisciplinary topic that combines computer science, statistics, and a social context. It is giving meaning to numbers. Data can be used to represent social behavior through a quantifiable means. We use this understanding to make decisions based on the raw data. Data is gathered everywhere. From our phones tracking our location and scroll patterns. To grocery stores tracking what products are purchased when. Data is constantly being collected with or without our knowledge. These everyday interactions get collected and stored. Data is used to create profiles and find patterns that companies can then use to target audiences. It is our job as data scientists to understand how the data is gathered, used and manipulated. We have to question the ethics and implementations of data. We can not treat data as merely raw numbers but understand the context as a whole. Behind all data are real humans with complex information the data can not store. We need to understand this context to truly use the data. This brings ethical responsibility to our job, we must use data without causing harm. Biased data sets lead to discrimination. Data can encode for something deeper than what we collect, leading to vulnerable populations being targeted. We need to know what data is ethical and useful to collect and what data can harm what populations. In this course I hope to deepen my understanding of the benefits of data as well as strengthen my knowledge on ethics. I am a double major with psychology and I think the human perspective of data is incredibly interesting. I want to learn frameworks for determining what data is helpful vs harmful and how to balance these. I understand data is incredibly important and useful but at what cost.
